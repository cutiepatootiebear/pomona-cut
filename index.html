How Matin Family works

  • Log your weight whenever you weigh in. Once a day is plenty.
  • We don't score single days — daily 1–3 lb bounces are just water, salt, and sleep. We score your 30-day trend line, so the number is honest.
  • Positive % = trend heading down. That's the win.
  • Nobody eats under 1,200 calories. This is a game, not a crash diet.
  • Hit your goal? You switch to Maintaining 🛡 — staying in your zone counts as a win, every day.
  • Steady 1–2 lb a week is the kind that stays gone.
  • Be kind in the group. We're all on the same team.
